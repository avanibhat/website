About

Hi I'm Avani Bhat

I'm a data scientist with a background in Life Science Informatics, and I build AI systems for biomedical and clinical data. Most of my work sits where language models meet structured knowledge. I've built RAG pipelines and agentic workflows, exposed them as MCP servers so other LLM clients can use them, and evaluated them with LLM-as-judge frameworks. Before that, I validated Alzheimer's disease knowledge graphs at Fraunhofer SCAI, combined BERT embeddings with RDF graph embeddings for literature recommendation at ZB MED, and mapped 50,000 biomedical preprints at EMBO.

What I care about most is whether a model can be trusted. That means checking its outputs against sources, explaining its predictions, and building pipelines that someone else can rerun and get the same answer. I'm looking for research and applied roles in biomedical AI, LLM systems, and knowledge graphs, including PhD positions.

Projects

NeuroQuery — RAG System with LLM Evaluation

2025

A retrieval augmented generation system for Alzheimer's disease literature. BioBERT embeddings and ChromaDB handle retrieval over PubMed abstracts and local PDFs, and Llama 3.3 70B (via Groq) generates source cited answers, served through FastAPI and Docker. An LLM-as-judge framework scores retrieval precision@5, answer faithfulness, and completeness, and compares dense against sparse retrieval.

Agentic Data Analyst

2026

An autonomous agent built with LangGraph (ReAct pattern) and Llama 3.3 70B that takes any tabular dataset, profiles it, selects and runs statistical tests, detects anomalies, generates plots, and writes a structured report without human input. Pancreas Fate Validation — Single-Cell Trajectories

2026

Tests whether CellRank 2 fate drivers inferred from RNA velocity agree with known regulators of mouse pancreatic endocrine development (scVelo, 3,696 cells). Known regulators such as Arx (Alpha) and Hhex (Delta) rank first for their lineages. Also compares TF expression against CollecTRI regulatory activity (decoupler) and documents limitations, including a Beta fate sink bias and anchor set sensitivity.
